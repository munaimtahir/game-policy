# Privacy Policy **Effective date:** 2 June 2026 This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game **Arcade Trio: Offline Games** published on Google Play. ## 1. Overview **Arcade Trio: Offline Games** is a lightweight offline arcade game. The core gameplay is designed to work without an internet connection, without account registration, and without requiring users to provide personal information. ## 2. Information We Collect In the current version of the app, we do not intentionally collect, store, sell, or share personal information from users. The app does not require: * Name * Email address * Phone number * Location * Account registration * Contacts * Photos or media access * Microphone access * Camera access * Health or financial information Game progress, scores, settings, and preferences may be stored locally on the user’s device. This local data is used only to make the app function properly, such as saving high scores, settings, or progress. This information is not sent to us. ## 3. Offline Gameplay The core gameplay of **Arcade Trio: Offline Games** is designed to work offline. Internet access is not required for basic gameplay. ## 4. Children’s Privacy The app is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken. ## 5. Advertising and In-App Purchases This Privacy Policy must match the actual version of the app uploaded to Google Play. If the uploaded version of the app does not contain advertisements, analytics, crash reporting, or in-app purchases, then no such data processing is performed by this version. If advertisements, analytics, crash reporting, or in-app purchases are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety section will be updated before or at the time of release to describe the relevant third-party services and data handling practices. ## 6. Third-Party Services The current version of the app does not require users to log in to any third-party service. If future versions include third-party services such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or billing, those services may collect information according to their own privacy policies. This policy will be updated to identify those services if they are added. ## 7. Data Sharing We do not sell user data. In the current version, we do not share personal user data with third parties because we do not intentionally collect personal user data. ## 8. Data Security Because the current version does not intentionally collect personal information, we do not maintain a user database for the app. Local game data stored on the user’s device remains on the device unless removed by the user by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. ## 9. User Choices Users may remove locally stored game data by: * Clearing the app’s data from Android settings, or * Uninstalling the app ## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes, especially if new features, third-party SDKs, advertisements, analytics, crash reporting, cloud services, or in-app purchases are added. The updated Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. ## 11.
